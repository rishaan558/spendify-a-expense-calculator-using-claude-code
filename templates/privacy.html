Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Spendly is a personal expense tracker, so almost everything in it is information about your own money. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do — and don't do — with it.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what the service needs to work:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Technical data — basic log information such as your browser type and the time of a request, used to keep the service running and secure.

We do not connect to your bank and we never ask for card numbers, bank credentials, or other payment details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to provide the service back to you — to display your transactions, calculate monthly totals, build category breakdowns, and filter your spending by date range.

We also use it to keep your account secure, respond to support requests, and diagnose problems with the app. We do not sell your data, rent it out, or use your expense records to target advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and kept for as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never as plain text.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, but no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You can delete individual expenses at any time, and deleting your account removes your associated records.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of outside providers to operate — for example hosting infrastructure and the web font service used to load the typefaces on these pages. These providers may process limited technical data, such as your IP address, purely to deliver what they serve.

We share your expense data with third parties only where it is necessary to run the service, or where we are required to by law. Third party providers have their own privacy practices, which their policies describe.

5. Your Choices

You can view, correct, or delete the information in your account at any time from within the app. If you would like a copy of your data or want your account removed entirely, contact us and we will help.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service develops. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app or by email.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or about how your information is handled, get in touch through the support options in your account. You may also want to read our Terms and Conditions.
